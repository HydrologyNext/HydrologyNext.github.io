Flagship

A digital twin of the water cycle, at 1 km and 1 day

DTE Hydrology Next, which we coordinate for ESA, rebuilds Europe’s water cycle from satellite data: rainfall, soil moisture, evaporation, water storage and river discharge, all in one consistent picture. Twelve institutes across Europe build it with us, with pilots in Africa and Central America.

Research

The water cycle, measured from space

We follow water through every stage of its journey, pairing each with the satellites that see it and a model we build ourselves, so an orbiting measurement becomes a number someone can act on.

  1. 01 / 05

    Precipitation

    Rain from space, worked backwards: estimating rainfall from soil moisture (SM2RAIN) and water storage (TWS2RAIN), and building the HYPER-P product.

    HYPER-P · SM2RAIN · TWS2RAIN

    Paolo Filippucci, Muhammad Usman Liaqat, Sindhu Kalimisetty

  2. 02 / 05

    Soil moisture

    The water the land holds: mapping irrigation and groundwater recharge, extremes, flash droughts and drought-to-flood transitions.

    ASCAT · Sentinel-1 · SMAP · ESA CCI · NISAR

    Jacopo Dari, Jaime Gaona Garcia, Vaibhav Kumar, Imane Serbouti, Margherita Raffaella Blois

  3. 03 / 05

    Evaporation

    Water returning to the sky: assimilating evaporation, soil moisture and storage into MISDc to quantify how people alter the cycle.

    GLEAM · MISDc

    Rubina Ansari, Sindhu Kalimisetty

  4. 04 / 05

    Groundwater and gravity

    Water we cannot see: weighing terrestrial water storage, preparing next-generation gravity missions, and testing an AMOC collapse.

    GRACE · NGGM · MAGIC

    Muhammad Usman Liaqat, Francesco Leopardi, Kay Khaing Kyaw

  5. 05 / 05

    Rivers

    Water on the move: river discharge and runoff from optical and altimetry data, filling the gaps between ground gauges.

    Optical · Altimetry · STREAM

    Paolo Filippucci, Francesco Leopardi

Impact

Science for when water matters most

Floods

Wet soil decides whether rain becomes a flood. We track it everywhere, so forecasters see the danger building before the river does.

H SAF · OEMC

Droughts

Flash droughts can dry a landscape in weeks. We catch them early across Europe and India, and show where they are heading in a warming climate.

OEMC · HydroDataCube4EU

Irrigation and food

Nobody knows how much water agriculture really uses. We measure it from orbit, field by field, so water can be shared fairly.

Irrigation-EU · CCI AWU IMPRES

Water and climate

A digital twin of the terrestrial water cycle, at a kilometre and a day, to test how human use and a changing ocean reshape Europe’s water.

Event · 7–9 October 2026 · Barcelona

OEMC final project meeting in Barcelona

Hydrology Next joined partners of the Open-Earth-Monitor (OEMC) project in Barcelona to close four years of work together. The team presented results on satellite soil moisture, flash droughts across Europe, and how Earth observation is starting to outperform models in drought monitoring.

Project: OEMC · European Commission, 2022–2026

Event7–11 September 2026

ESA Advanced Training Course on the water cycle comes to Perugia

ESA’s 15th Advanced Training Course on Land Remote Sensing brought 50 students to the University of Perugia for a week on precipitation, soil moisture, irrigation, snow, river discharge, evaporation and water storage. Jacopo Dari and Francesco Leopardi were on the organising committee, with CNR among the partners. Course materials are freely available.
