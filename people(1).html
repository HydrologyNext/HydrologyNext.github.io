Leadership

Luca Brocca

Director of Research · Group leader

Institute of Geosciences, National Research Council of Italy (CNR-GEO) · Perugia

Leads the group’s work on new hydrological applications of Earth observation, across every satellite product and model. Involved in all 13 active projects and coordinator of DTE Hydrology Next and Irrigation-EU.

Born in Genoa in 1978, Luca earned an MSc in Environmental Engineering (2003) and a PhD in Civil Engineering (2008), both from the University of Perugia. He joined CNR in Perugia in 2003, became a Researcher in 2009 and Director of Research in 2019, and heads the Hydrology Next group at the institute renamed CNR-GEO in July 2026. He has authored about 250 journal papers and released more than 50 open datasets of soil moisture, irrigation and precipitation, including SM2RAIN and HYPER-P.

  • Highly Cited Researcher, 2019 and 2020
  • ESA–EGU Excellence Award to the group, 2021
  • Science Manager, EUMETSAT H SAF
  • Co-editor-in-chief, Earth Observation

Our values

Diversity and inclusion

Science is real.Everyone is welcome.Feminism is for everyone.

Water connects every community on Earth, and our group should welcome all of them.

Good science needs many perspectives. We welcome people of every background, regardless of race, ethnicity, nationality, citizenship or migration status, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability or socioeconomic background. The questions we study, from droughts and floods to irrigation and water security, affect people everywhere, and a team that reflects that diversity asks better questions and builds tools that serve more people.

We recognize that academia has not always been equally open to everyone, and that inclusion takes active work, not only good intentions. We commit to examining our own biases, listening to the experiences of every group member, and changing what is not working.
